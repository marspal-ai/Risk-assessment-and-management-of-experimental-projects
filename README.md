# Risk-assessment-and-management-of-experimental-projects实验项目风险评估管理

#### 系统介绍

实验项目风险评估系统通过建立实验室环境与实验活动的科学评估机制，实现对实验项目全过程的风险识别与防控。支持自定义评估部门、审批流程及专家组管理，可对项目进行立项申请、专家评审与多级审核，实现“风险预判—专家评估—分级管控”的闭环管理。系统支持灵活配置评审字段，评审结果可导出Excel，全面提升实验人员安全意识与实验室安全管理水平，推动隐患治理规范化、科学化。

#### English version introduction

#### System Introduction

The experimental project risk assessment system establishes a scientific evaluation mechanism for laboratory environment and experimental activities to identify and prevent risks throughout the entire process of experimental projects. Support custom evaluation departments, approval processes, and expert group management, enabling project initiation applications, expert reviews, and multi-level audits, achieving a closed-loop management of "risk prediction expert evaluation hierarchical control". The system supports flexible configuration of review fields, and the review results can be exported to Excel, comprehensively improving the safety awareness of laboratory personnel and the level of laboratory safety management, and promoting the standardization and scientification of hazard management.
